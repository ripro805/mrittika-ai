# Agronomic Domain Compliance & BARC Standards

## 1. Compliance Mandates
Mrittika AI adheres strictly to the official agricultural recommendations of the People's Republic of Bangladesh:
- **BARC (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council)**: *Fertilizer Recommendation Guide (FRG 2024)*.
- **BRRI (Bangladesh Rice Research Institute)**: Modern rice cultivation practices, nutrient schedules for Aus, Aman, and Boro.
- **BARI (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute)**: High-yield vegetable and field crop production manuals.
- **BMD (Bangladesh Meteorological Department)**: 30 Agro-Ecological Zone (AEZ) seasonal baselines.

---

## 2. Soil Nutrients & Climatological Boundaries
All user inputs are validated against rigorous agronomic bounds:
- **Nitrogen ($N$)**: $0 - 300\text{ kg/ha}$
- **Phosphorus ($P$)**: $0 - 200\text{ kg/ha}$
- **Potassium ($K$)**: $0 - 300\text{ kg/ha}$
- **Soil Acidity ($\text{pH}$)**: $3.5 - 9.5$ (Values below 5.5 trigger liming advisory; values above 8.0 trigger sulfur/gypsum advisory).
- **Temperature**: $5.0 - 50.0^\circ\text{C}$
- **Relative Humidity**: $10.0 - 100.0\%$
- **Rainfall**: $0.0 - 1200.0\text{ mm}$

---

## 3. Split Application Timing Guidelines
1. **Basal Stage (জমি তৈরির শেষ চাষ)**: 100% TSP, 100% Gypsum, 100% Zinc Sulfate, and 50% MoP thoroughly mixed into the puddle layer.
2. **First Top Dressing (১ম কিস্তি উপরিপ্রয়োগ)**: 1/3 of total Urea applied 15-20 days after transplanting (active tillering stage) under saturated soil conditions.
3. **Second Top Dressing (২য় কিস্তি উপরিপ্রয়োগ)**: 1/3 of total Urea applied 35-40 days after transplanting (maximum tillering stage).
4. **Third Top Dressing (৩য় কিস্তি উপরিপ্রয়োগ)**: Remaining 1/3 of Urea and 50% of MoP applied 5-7 days before panicle initiation (কাইচ থোড় আসার ৫-৭ দিন আগে).
